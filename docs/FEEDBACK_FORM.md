- Owned by the project's Google account. Built on 2026-11-06 by the Apps Script project "Trading Floor feedback form builder" in that account; running it again makes a second, separate form.
- Emails are not collected automatically. Testers can leave one if they want a follow-up.

## Questions
**What you tried**
1. Which parts did you try? (checkboxes, required): The Floor feed, Posted a trade, Replays, News, Explore, Built or followed a strategy, Followed another trader, Settings / private account, iPhone app
2. What did you use it on? (one choice, required): iPhone app, iPhone browser, Android browser, Laptop or desktop, Tablet

**How it felt**
3. How easy was it to sign up and set up your profile? (1-5, required)
4. How easy was it to post a trade? (1-5, optional)
5. How useful would The Trading Floor be for your own trading? (1-5, required)
6. How do you like the look of the site? (1-5)
7. What did you like most? (paragraph)
8. What was confusing or frustrating? (paragraph)
9. Did anything break or look wrong? (paragraph; asks for what they did just before, and device and browser)

**What next**
10. Which should we build next? (one choice + Other): Notifications, Direct messages, Verified P/L from a broker, Leaderboards, Import trades from a broker
11. How likely are you to recommend it to another trader? (0-10, required)
12. Can we contact you about your answers? (Yes/No, required)
13. If yes, your email (short answer, validated as an email)
