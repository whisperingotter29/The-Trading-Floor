Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 September 2026

The Trading Floor is a social platform where day traders post their trades and publish the strategies behind them. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

Who runs this site

The Trading Floor is an independent project operated by its owner, based in the Philippines. For any privacy question, or to ask for your data or account to be deleted, email whisperingotter29@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address, and if you sign in with Google, the name and profile information Google shares with us. We never see or store your Google password.
  • Your profile. The display name, handle, three-letter badge, badge colour, markets and bio you choose.
  • What you post. Screenshots and screen recordings you upload, the profit or loss and risk-to-reward figures you enter, captions, comments, and the strategies you publish.
  • Your activity. Which posts you like, save or comment on, and which traders and strategies you follow.
  • Basic technical data. Our hosting and database providers keep standard server logs, which can include your IP address and browser type, to keep the service running and secure.

We do not ask for or want your brokerage login, account numbers, or any payment details. Please do not put them in a screenshot or caption.

How we use it

Your information is used to run the platform: to sign you in, show your posts to other traders, display your profile, count likes and follows, and keep the site working and free of abuse. We do not sell your data, and we do not run advertising.

What other people can see

Assume anything you post is public. Your profile, posts, uploaded media, captions, comments, published strategies and the trade figures you enter can be seen by anyone on the internet, including people without an account.

Your email address is never shown to other users. The posts you save are private to you.

Where your data is stored

The platform runs on two service providers who process data on our behalf:

  • Supabase stores accounts, posts and uploaded files. Our database is hosted in the Asia Pacific (Sydney) region.
  • Vercel hosts and serves the website.

If you sign in with Google, Google handles that sign-in under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

  • Delete a post. You can delete any post you made at any time, which also removes the uploaded file.
  • Delete your account. Email us and we will delete your account, your posts, your uploads and your strategies. We will confirm once it is done.
  • Get a copy of your data. Email us and we will send you the information we hold about you.
  • Correct your details. You can edit your profile at any time.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over your personal data, including under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 and, for visitors in Europe or the UK, the GDPR. We will honour those requests regardless of where you are.

How long we keep things

We keep your account and posts until you delete them or ask us to delete your account. Server logs held by our providers are kept for a limited period under their own policies.

Children

This platform is not intended for anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, please contact us and we will remove it.

Security

Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth, so we never store passwords ourselves. Database rules mean you can only edit or delete your own content. No service can promise perfect security, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be announced on the site.
